(｡♥‿♥｡) About Me ♡( ◡‿◡ )

Hello there! I am Samantha Erin B. Aquino, a 2nd Year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student currently residing in the Philippines. 🌸

I’m currently studying at Asia Pacific College (APC) located at

3 Humabon Place, Magallanes Village, Makati City, Metro Manila, Philippines (1232)
. I am passionately working towards finishing my bachelor’s degree, and I am honored to currently hold 3 scholarships that actively support my tuition and academic endeavors! 🎓✨

Why BSIT? I chose Information Technology because I am acutely aware of how incredibly fast-paced technology evolves every single day. Asia Pacific College is recognized as a credited Center of Excellence in IT, making it the perfect home institution to fulfill my scholarship standards and future ambitions.

₍ᐢ. ̫.ᐢ₎ IT Experience & Technical Journey ⌨️ ✧*̥˚

As far as my IT experience goes, I believe in getting hands-on with both code, design, and systems:

  • Mini Hackathons & College Orgs: Actively joined hackathons organized by student bodies like JPCS-APC (Junior Philippine Computer Society) and AWS student community groups.
  • Database Administration: Hands-on experience managing and administering student and organizational officer databases.
  • Game Programming (DASTRUC Finals): Collaborated closely with peers to design, architect, and code an interactive game applying Data Structures and Algorithms concepts.

💡 "For me, writing or generating code is the straightforward part — efficient debugging is the real art and challenge. My aspiration is to shift from simply decorating a webpage to making it think algorithmically, solving bugs with confidence and bringing fun, impactful software to life!"
