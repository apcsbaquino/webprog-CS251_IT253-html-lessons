I’m currently studying at Asia Pacific College (APC) located at

3 Humabon Place, Magallanes Village, Makati City, Metro Manila, Philippines (1232)

Asia Pacific College is recognized as a credited Center of Excellence in IT, making it the perfect home institution to fulfill my scholarship standards and future ambitions.
